This game has no personal phones. Every device is a stationary station terminal placed in one room of the house — that's where you join, see your role, do tasks, sabotage, call a Red Alert, and vote.

Setting one up? Go to /station.html — it assigns itself a room automatically, nothing to pick.

Running the game? The control screen is at /host.html.
